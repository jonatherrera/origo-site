The spring dip and the opportunity

In April the site dipped in the number of search terms it ranks for. This lines up with a Google algorithm update, not a loss of the site's standing, and in fact the quality links pointing to the site grew 41% over the same period. The cleanup we completed this year is exactly what makes this easy to recover, and turning it back around is the focus of the next phase.

The clearest quick win is getting more people to click. The site shows up in Google over 88,000 times a month, but only about 1.46% of the people who see it actually click through. Getting that to a normal 3% would more than double the traffic, without ranking for anything new.
