Something We Found While Reviewing Your Site

There is a hidden SEO issue on your current website. No charge to flag it.

While reviewing pocketplumberllc.com in preparation for this proposal, we spotted something buried in your site's raw HTML code: a reference to "Nashville Electric Pros," a leftover placeholder from a House Call Pro template that was never cleaned up. It does not show on the visible page, but Google reads the raw code.

That means Googlebot may be picking up a competing brand name on your pages, which dilutes the relevance signals you are building for Pocket Plumber LLC in San Antonio. It is not a crisis. But it is the kind of thing that quietly works against you, and it is exactly what happens when your platform is managed by someone who treats your site like a template instead of your business.

We caught it because we looked closely. That is what you should expect from whoever is in your corner.
