SEO & AEO FAQ

Questions, answered.

What's the difference between SEO and AEO?

SEO gets you ranking in traditional search results like Google. AEO, answer engine optimization, gets you recognized and cited by AI answer engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google's AI Overviews. They overlap, but the newer one needs its own strategy.

How does Origo's SEO process work?

We start with a full audit, then build a prioritized roadmap. We fix the technical foundation first, then publish content and optimize your pages and profile, and report on progress every month. You'll always know what's happening.

How long does SEO take to show results?

It depends on your market and starting point, but meaningful movement usually takes a few months, and compounds from there. 360 Collision's biggest gains came across a full year. Anyone promising instant results isn't being honest.

Do I need a new website to do SEO with Origo?

Not always. If your current site can be updated and its foundation is sound, we work with it. If it's holding you back in ways that can't be fixed, we'll tell you honestly and talk through options.

Will Origo guarantee a #1 ranking?

No, and you should be wary of anyone who does. Rankings depend on factors outside anyone's control. What we do commit to is honest, sustained work and clear reporting, the things that actually move rankings over time.

Does Origo do local SEO?

Yes, and for owner-operated businesses it's often the biggest lever. Google Business Profile was 360 Collision's single largest lead driver. We manage local presence so nearby searches turn into calls and directions.
