Web Design FAQ

Questions, answered.

+How long does a website project take?

Most sites take four to six weeks from kickoff to launch, depending on the number of pages and how quickly we get your content and feedback. We'll give you a clear timeline on the discovery call.

+Do you write the content too?

We can. Great design needs great words. We'll either build around copy you provide or bring in our copywriting service so the writing works as hard as the design does.

+Can I update the site myself after launch?

Yes. We build on a system you can manage and walk you through it before handoff. Change text, swap photos, and add pages, no developer needed. And if you'd rather not touch it, our Website Concierge service can handle updates and upkeep for you.

+Do you handle hosting and the domain?

We'll set up hosting, connect your domain, and make sure everything's secure and backed up. You own all of it, no strings attached.

+Will my site work on phones?

Every site we build is designed mobile-first and tested across phones, tablets, and desktops before it goes live. That's the whole point of how we work.

+What if I already have a site?

We start from what's working and rebuild the rest. Sometimes that's a refresh, sometimes a clean start. We'll be honest about which one you actually need.

+Do you help with SEO?

Every site we build sits on a clean, search-friendly structure. For deeper keyword and content work, our SEO & AEO service picks up right where the build leaves off.
