The problem

A logo isn't a brand. And a bad brand costs you more than you think.

Most small businesses have a logo they've outgrown, colors they chose randomly, and no consistent way to present themselves. The result? They look like every other company in their industry, and prospects don't remember them.

Inconsistent visuals that look unprofessional across platforms.

No clear brand voice, messaging that sounds generic or off.

A logo that doesn't scale, print well, or work in different contexts.

Prospects can't tell what makes you different from the competition.

You're embarrassed to hand out your business card or share your website.

Our approach

A brand system built to communicate, not just decorate.

We start with strategy, who you are, who you serve, and what makes you different. Then we build a visual and verbal identity system that expresses that clearly and consistently across every touchpoint.

Strategy first

We listen before we design.

The right idea comes from understanding your business, not from a trend. Positioning and voice come before a single pixel.

Built to work

Not pretty. Effective.

The goal is a brand that attracts the right clients, builds trust faster, and makes every piece of your marketing stronger.

Branding FAQ

Questions, answered.

+What's included in an Origo branding project?

Every project is scoped to your business, but most include brand discovery, a full logo system, a color palette, a type system, a voice guide, and a complete brand style guide. We'll walk you through exactly what's right for you on the discovery call.

+Do I get a logo or a full brand identity?

A full identity. A logo on its own can't carry a brand. You get the marks plus the colors, type, voice, and guidelines that make them work together everywhere.

+How many logo concepts will I see?

We present two distinct creative directions, each with its own rationale, then refine the one you choose. We'd rather go deep on two strong ideas than thin on ten.

+What if I already have a logo I like?

Then we build around it. If it's working, we'll keep it and develop the system it's missing. If it's holding you back, we'll tell you honestly and talk through options.

+Do I own the final brand files?

Yes. Every final file is yours, in every format you'll need for digital and print. No licensing games, no holding your brand hostage.

+Can Origo handle printed materials too?

Yes. Business cards, signage, packaging, and more. We design your brand to print as well as it shows up on screen.

+How long does a branding project take?

Most run three to five weeks from kickoff to delivery, depending on scope and how quickly we get feedback.
