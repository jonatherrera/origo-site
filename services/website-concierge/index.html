Website Concierge FAQ

Questions, answered.

+What is Website Concierge?

It's ongoing care for your website. Every month we handle updates, backups, security, monitoring, and small content edits, so your site stays healthy and you never have to touch it or wait on a developer.

+Why is Website Concierge a 12-month agreement?

Real maintenance is a relationship, not a one-off. Twelve months gives us time to actually improve your site, not just keep the lights on, and it keeps your monthly cost low and predictable.

+What's included in Website Concierge?

That depends on your plan. Every plan covers updates, backups, and monitoring. Higher plans add security, speed work, more content-edit hours, SEO tweaks, and faster response times. See the plans above for the full breakdown.

+How fast does Origo respond to update requests?

On Growth we respond the same business day. On Premium you get a 4-hour response SLA. Most small edits are live the same day you ask.

+What if I need a major change to my website?

Small edits are part of your plan. For a larger project, like a new section or a redesign, we'll scope it separately and pick up right where your plan leaves off.

+Do I have to use Website Concierge if Origo built my site?

Not at all. Every site we build is yours to run. Concierge is for owners who'd simply rather we keep it maintained, and we're happy to take over a site we didn't build too.

+Can I cancel or change plans?

You can move up or down a plan as your needs change. After the initial 12 months, Concierge continues month to month, and your site and everything we've done stays yours.
